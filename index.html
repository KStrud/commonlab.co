Free creative help* for people working for the common good.* from a human!

In times like these, we all need to do our bit to help. Creativity can be a great power for change but what's even greater are the humans that offer up their time to help people and planet. This studio aims to bring the two together, volunteering creative time to help supercharge the work being done to make the world a better place.

01

Who's behind this?

I'm Kate, a multi-disciplinary designer with over 10 years of experience across medicine, forensic science, print design and tech. It's currently just me, but I'm hoping to grow.

02

Why are you doing this?

The world is feeling a little messy right now and I want to use my skills to help. It's as simple as that.

03

Who do you work with?

Anyone doing something to make the world a better place — a startup working on the environment, an individual with a community idea, a non-profit a little tight on money. If you're doing something to help and need creative support but can't afford to hire yet, I'm here. Not sure if you qualify? Just reach out.

04

Is it really free?

Yes — but my time isn't unlimited. I offer my time for free, but any costs associated with hosting, printing, or asset creation are on you.

05

Can I see your work?

Sure — reach out and I'll share what's relevant. Or take a look at katestrudwick.com.
